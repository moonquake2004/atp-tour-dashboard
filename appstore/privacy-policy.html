隐私政策

最后更新 / Last updated: 2026-09-16
本 app 不收集你的任何个人数据。没有账号，没有分析 SDK，没有广告。

我们收集什么

不收集。本 app：

  • 不需要注册或登录，没有账号系统
  • 不收集姓名、邮箱、电话、通讯录、照片、位置、设备标识符
  • 不集成任何第三方分析、广告或崩溃上报 SDK
  • 不追踪你在其他 app 或网站上的活动

数据存在哪里

你主动添加的内容（例如收藏的球员、界面语言偏好）只保存在你的设备本地。 删除 app 即随之删除。我们无法访问这些内容。

网络请求

app 需要联网以获取最新数据。请求会发往：

  • 本 app 的数据来源服务器（用于获取排名、赛程与赛果）
  • 图片服务器（用于加载球员头像）

这些请求不携带任何可识别你个人身份的信息，不含账号、不含设备标识符。 与所有网络请求一样，接收方服务器会看到你的 IP 地址——这是互联网通信的必要组成部分， 我们无法代其控制。

推送通知（如启用）

  • iOS 会提供一个由苹果生成的推送令牌，仅用于向你的设备投递通知
  • 该令牌不与你的身份关联，我们不做用户画像
  • 你可随时在 iOS「设置」中关闭通知，或在 app 内关闭

儿童

本 app 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

你的权利

由于我们不收集个人数据，因此不存在需要导出或删除的个人数据档案。 如需删除本地数据，移除 app 即可。

政策变更

政策如有更新，我们会修改本页顶部的「最后更新」日期，并在 app 内呈现最新版本。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-16
This app collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics SDKs and no advertising.

What we collect

Nothing. This app:

  • requires no registration or sign-in and has no account system
  • does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, location or device identifiers
  • integrates no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs
  • does not track your activity across other apps or websites

Where your data lives

Anything you add yourself — for example favourite players or your language preference — is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. We have no way to access it.

Network requests

The app goes online to fetch current data. Requests are made to:

  • the data source servers the app is built on, to retrieve rankings, calendars and results
  • image servers, to load player portraits

These requests carry no personally identifying information — no account, no device identifier. As with any network request, the receiving server sees your IP address, which is inherent to how the internet works and is outside our control.

Push notifications (if you enable them)

  • iOS provides a push token generated by Apple, used solely to deliver notifications to your device
  • the token is not linked to your identity and is not used to profile you
  • you can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings or in the app

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is no personal data profile to export or delete. To remove local data, delete the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update the "Last updated" date above and present the current version in the app.
